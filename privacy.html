Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

This policy explains how information may be processed when you use AERONEX, a Discord bot project for virtual airline and aviation communities.

1. Information That May Be Processed

Depending on the features enabled and how the bot is configured, AERONEX may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, flight schedule details, recruitment records, and command-related information needed to perform requested actions.

Do not assume every listed data type is collected in every deployment. The operator should verify this list against the deployed code and configuration.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide flight scheduling, display operational updates, manage configured recruitment workflows, check permissions, assign roles when authorized, and maintain records needed for the bot's functions.

3. Storage and Retention

Flight records may be stored in a SQLite database in the hosting environment. Other records, logs, and data may have different storage and retention behaviour depending on the deployed code and hosting configuration.

Data is retained only according to the actual operation and configured retention process. No specific deletion period is promised by this policy unless the operator verifies and documents it.

4. Sharing and Service Providers

The bot may rely on Discord and a hosting provider to operate. Information may be processed by those services as required for their functions and subject to their own privacy policies. The operator should accurately disclose any additional data sharing that takes place.

5. Security

Reasonable measures should be used to protect bot credentials, configuration, databases, and access to stored information. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Never publish bot tokens or private credentials.

6. Your Requests

For questions or requests concerning information handled by the bot, contact the operator using the email below. Requests may need to identify the relevant Discord server or records so they can be located. Applicable legal rights and obligations may vary by location.

7. Children's Privacy

The operator should assess applicable age requirements and laws before making the service available. Do not use AERONEX to collect unnecessary sensitive personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the service or its data handling changes. The effective date above should be revised whenever a material update is published.
