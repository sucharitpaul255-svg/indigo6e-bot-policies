Privacy Policy

INDIGO 6E Flight Schedule Bot · Effective date: 12 October 2026

Scope: This policy is drafted from the Bot setup described by its operator: a Discord community flight-schedule bot hosted on Railway with a SQLite database and intended automatic cleanup of flight records around four hours after scheduled departure. The operator must verify actual code, logging, backups, and provider settings before treating every statement as definitive.

1. Information the Bot may process

To operate its Discord features, the Bot may process:

  • Discord user, server (guild), channel, and role identifiers used to execute commands, check staff permissions, or send announcements;
  • flight details entered by authorized users, such as flight number, route, scheduled departure time, gate, aircraft, status, and announcement text;
  • technical information or error output available in runtime logs to diagnose problems and protect the service.

The Bot is not intended to request passwords, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or sensitive personal information. Please do not submit such information through the Bot.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to provide flight scheduling, validate staff permissions, publish flight and operations announcements, maintain flight records, troubleshoot errors, and help prevent misuse.

3. Storage, hosting, and retention

The described setup stores flight records in a SQLite database hosted on Railway. The intended cleanup behavior removes flight records approximately four hours after their scheduled departure time. Actual deletion depends on the deployed Bot code and whether its cleanup process is running correctly. Messages posted to Discord may remain in Discord unless deleted by the Bot or a server administrator. Technical logs, backups, or provider records may follow separate retention settings; the operator should verify these settings before making more specific promises.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. Discord processes information when you interact with the Bot or view messages it sends. Railway provides hosting and may process information as necessary to run the Bot and store its database. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or the operator.

5. Permissions and security

The Bot may use configured staff roles and Discord permissions to restrict management commands. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Server owners should limit staff roles and channel access to trusted people and keep Bot credentials confidential.

6. Your choices and requests

You may stop using the Bot. You may ask the relevant Discord server administrators to remove messages they control. For questions or requests about information stored by the Bot, contact the operator at palsuchar@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed subject to technical limitations, legitimate operational needs, and applicable law.

7. Children and age requirements

The Bot is not specifically designed to collect information from children. Users must comply with Discord’s minimum age requirements and applicable local law.

8. Third-party services

Discord and Railway are third-party services and have their own terms and privacy practices. This policy does not replace those providers’ policies.

9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Bot or its data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a revised version is published.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact palsuchar@gmail.com.
